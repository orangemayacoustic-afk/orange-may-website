Our Story

It started with family.
Then came the music.

We're Cristian and Lara, the two voices behind Orange May.

Our story began almost by chance: our siblings are married to each other, which brought us into the same family long before it brought us onto the same stage.

What started simply became Orange May — an acoustic project built around songs people know, rearranged in the way we like to play them.

Guitar, two voices, percussion and live loops allow us to move from intimate acoustic moments to energetic nights, without losing the feeling of two musicians actually playing together.

We perform across hotels, venues, weddings and private events, adapting every set to the atmosphere in front of us.

Different places. Different nights.
Always live. Always Orange May.
